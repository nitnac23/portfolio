About Me

UX Architect at Salesforce

I am a Principal UX Architect with over 12 years of experience transforming complex, highly technical enterprise systems into intuitive, high-polish visual experiences. My superpower lies at the intersection of hands-on UI design craft and large-scale systems architecture. How I Work While many senior designers transition out of design files, I make a deliberate choice to stay hands-on. I believe the most impactful product strategies are forged directly in Figma, code, and design system components. Whether I am architecting multi-app environments across Experience Cloud, prototyping conversational interaction models for Agentforce AI, or solving extreme mobile accessibility constraints for low-literacy end users, I focus on delivering software that is as useful and accessible as it is beautiful. My Journey Over the past decade—spanning enterprise transformations at Salesforce, T-Mobile, and large-scale public sector initiatives—I’ve led end-to-end design strategies, built reusable design systems, and partnered closely with executive leadership to turn ambiguous vision into deployed software. Core Focus Areas: AI & Conversational UX: Establishing interaction patterns and human-in-the-loop workflows for enterprise agentic experiences. Design Systems & UI Craft: Building tokenized component libraries, high-fidelity prototypes, and responsive mobile/desktop interfaces. Inclusive Strategy: Designing for strict WCAG/508 compliance, zero-friction mobile workflows, and diverse user capabilities. When I'm not designing in Figma or testing workflows, I'm exploring emerging AI tooling, mentoring junior designers, and finding new ways to simplify complex software.
